(see also [NOTICE](NOTICE)). The **data** it shows are derived from the sources below, which keep
their own licences and terms: the Apache licence does not cover them. The NASA Space Apps terms

SARs*). It is not endorsed by NASA, ISRO or any data provider, and use of their data does not
imply endorsement.

Items marked *(verify)* are terms we could not confirm from the provider's own page; they are
used as openly published data, credited and linked, until confirmed.

## Software

The web app has no runtime dependencies. Development uses Playwright (Apache-2.0). The pipeline
uses NumPy (BSD-3-Clause) and, for real data, h5py, rasterio, earthaccess and asf_search under
their own open-source licences. The notebooks install their libraries in Google Colab at run time.
